Saar Sparky, also known as AUS and Topaki, is a member of the Clvbhouse community and a chairman of Ethanism.

He also uses the formal aliases AUS Productions and Topakien. An additional alias is censored on this page.

Saar Sparky speaks German and English fluently and is currently learning Soyspeak, a partially constructed language associated with online communities.
