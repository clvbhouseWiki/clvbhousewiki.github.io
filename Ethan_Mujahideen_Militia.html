The Ethan Mujahideen Militia (EMM) is a fictional Islamic militia allied with Ethanism. The group presents itself as a force devoted to the glory of Ethan and the advancement of its ideological cause. Its leadership, identity, and public image are closely associated with its leader, Grass the Grass.

The militia's visual identity combines a dark-green emblem with desert imagery. Its supporters use slogans, insignia, and exaggerated martial imagery as symbols of loyalty to Ethan.

History and background

Within the CLVBHOUSE fictional setting, the Ethan Mujahideen Militia emerged as a militant ally of Ethanism. Its stated purpose is to defend the movement's interests and promote the standing of Ethan among its supporters.

The group is depicted as an ideological organization rather than simply a collection of individuals. Its identity revolves around allegiance, religious symbolism, and a shared commitment to Ethanist goals.

Setting note: The militia and its described history belong to the fictional CLVBHOUSE Wiki setting.

Ideology

The EMM combines an Islamic identity with support for Ethanism. Its fictional supporters frame their allegiance to Ethan as a central part of the group's purpose. The slogan displayed at the top of this page reflects that exaggerated, in-universe devotion.

The group's identity is presented through a combination of religious references, Ethanist allegiance, military aesthetics, and online community humour. These elements define its public image within the setting.

Leadership

The militia is led by Grass the Grass, who serves as its central figure. The leader's name is associated with the group's identity and appears in descriptions of its leadership.

Beyond the leadership attributed to Grass the Grass, the organization's internal hierarchy is left unspecified in this article.

Symbols and identity

The official emblem is represented by the image file ETHAN_MUJAHIDEEN_MILITIA.png. Dark green is used throughout this page to complement the emblem, while the sky and sand colours provide a desert-inspired backdrop.

The militia's visual identity includes its emblem, the initials EMM, and its Ethanist slogan. These features distinguish it from other fictional organizations on the wiki.
