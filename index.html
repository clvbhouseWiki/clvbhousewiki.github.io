Clvbhouse Wiki, also known as Bieshi Wiki or Biwisi Wiki by detractors, is an English-language MediaWiki-based community-driven online encyclopedia featuring independently fact-checked and peer-reviewed information about Ethan and the Clvbhouse, imageboard culture, and many other topics. Clvbhouse Wiki was created by Saar Sparky on March 29, 2021, and is currently administered by no one i guess.

Clvbhouse Wiki began as a way to document the mysterious activities of Ethan, the Clvbhouse population, and whatever was happening in the group chat that week. It later expanded to include historical records, questionable biographies, community lore, and things that probably should have stayed in the group chat.

To get started, see Clvbhouse Wiki:Portals. If you are lost, that is considered a normal user experience.

Influence

Clvbhouse Wiki has occasionally been used as a source by people who were too lazy to ask Ethan directly. Its articles have been cited in arguments, group chats, and at least one conversation that should probably have ended much earlier.

The encyclopedia is popular among individuals with too much free time and access to a keyboard. Readers frequently stumble across an article while searching for something completely unrelated, then spend the next forty minutes learning information that has absolutely no practical application.

It is occasionally used as a source on absolutely nothing, and by Google's AI on terms that nobody remembers searching.

Community

The Clvbhouse Wiki community consists of readers, editors, alleged experts, professional arguers, and individuals who have mistaken the edit button for a legally binding instrument.

Disagreements are generally resolved through discussion, increasingly long paragraphs, and the timeless academic method of saying "source: trust me bro".

Administration

At present, the wiki is administered by no one i guess. This arrangement has been described as both democratic freedom and an administrative emergency.
