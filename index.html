Clvbhouse Wiki, also known as Bieshi Wiki or Biwisi Wiki by its detractors, is an English-language community-driven encyclopedia documenting Ethan, the Clvbhouse community, imageboard culture, and the ideologies and events associated with them.

Its principal ideological subject is Ethanism, a right-wing ideology created around the glorification of Ethan. According to community lore, Ethanism helped make Ethan more popular and shaped much of the Clvbhouse community's identity.

The wiki also documents community leadership, operations intended to grow the server, and the label Nethanist, used by Ethanists for people they regard as opponents of Ethan or Clvbhouse.

Influence

Ethanism grew from the Clvbhouse community's repeated praise and exaggerated celebration of Ethan. What began as a running joke developed into its own terminology, symbols, flag, and ideological identity. The claim that Ethanism contributed to Ethan's popularity is part of the community's own account.

Operations

The community has described three central objectives: converting the Ethan Clubhouse comment section to Ethanism, convincing Ethan to upload more content, and growing the server. These are community-stated goals, not independently verified outcomes.

Nethanists

A Nethanist is a person described by Ethanists as opposing Ethan or the Clvbhouse. Community examples include TADC fans, Jak Does Snacks fans, and people who dislike Ethan. These are community-defined labels; liking a show or creator does not automatically mean someone opposes Ethanism.

Leadership

The community identifies Camemac and ARB as leaders, and Saar Sparky and Sancho as chairmen.
