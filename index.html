Clvbhouse Wiki, also known as Bieshi Wiki or Biwisi Wiki by its detractors, is an English-language community-driven online encyclopedia documenting Ethan, the Clvbhouse community, imageboard culture, and the various events, personalities, and ideologies associated with them. Its editorial standards are best described as ambitious, questionable, and occasionally conducted through sheer glazing.

The wiki's principal ideological subject is Ethanism, a community-created ideology originating from the repeated glorification of an individual known as Ethan. What began as an ongoing joke about Ethan developed into its own collection of terminology, beliefs, operations, supporters, opponents, and increasingly elaborate claims about his supposed significance.

Ethanism is closely associated with Clvbhouse culture. Its supporters discuss Ethan with a degree of enthusiasm that has occasionally raised the question of whether the community is joking, serious, or simply incapable of changing the topic.

Clvbhouse Wiki exists to document Ethanism, preserve community lore, and catalogue the people, activities, and disputes that have emerged from the Clvbhouse community.

Influence

Ethanism developed within the Clvbhouse community through the repeated praise, discussion, and exaggerated celebration of Ethan. Over time, the joke acquired its own terminology and ideological identity.

Its culture includes community operations, discussions about recruiting new supporters, attempts to encourage Ethan to upload content, and efforts to increase the size of the server. The importance assigned to these activities varies according to the enthusiasm of the participants involved.

Ethanism

Ethanism is an ideology created within the Clvbhouse community around the glorification of Ethan. Its defining characteristic is the exaggerated importance assigned to Ethan and the community's attempts to turn an ongoing joke into a broader ideological identity.

Ethanism has developed its own terminology, supporters, perceived opponents, and community objectives. Whether individual participants genuinely subscribe to its principles or are simply continuing the joke remains unclear.

In practice, Ethanism combines community identity, humorous ideological claims, online promotion, and an unusual degree of attention devoted to one individual.

Operations

The Ethanist community has described several operations intended to increase the ideology's reach and encourage greater participation. The stated objectives include the following:

  • Conversion of the Ethan Clubhouse comment section: Attempts to persuade commenters to take an interest in Ethanism and the Clvbhouse community.
  • Encouraging Ethan to upload: Convincing Ethan to publish more content.
  • Server growth: Increasing membership and participation within the Clvbhouse community.

These objectives reflect the community's stated ambitions rather than independently verified results. Their ultimate success remains a matter for future historians, or whoever is unfortunate enough to maintain the archive.

Nethanists

A Nethanist is a person described by the Ethanist community as opposing Ethan, Ethanism, or the culture associated with Clvbhouse. The term is used as a label for perceived ideological opponents.

According to the community's own descriptions, individuals who may be classified as Nethanists include:

  • Fans of The Amazing Digital Circus (TADC).
  • Fans of Jak Does Snacks.
  • People who dislike Ethan.

These categories represent the community's characterization of its opponents. Membership in any of these groups does not, by itself, establish that a person opposes Ethanism.

Nethanite boycott

The Nethanite boycott is a list circulated within the Ethanist community of businesses, media, and other subjects that supporters are encouraged to boycott. The community's stated list includes:

  • McDonald's.
  • Mickey's Clubhouse, alleged by the community to have copied Ethan.
  • Adult sexual content.
  • Israel, as specified in the community's boycott list.
  • Individuals accused by the community of betraying Ethanism.

The list reflects the community's own positions and grievances. Inclusion does not establish that any allegation is true or that every supporter follows the boycott.

Community

The Clvbhouse community consists of Ethanists, casual participants, observers, critics, and individuals whose level of commitment to the ideology is difficult to determine.

Community activity includes discussions of Ethanism, promotion of the server, attempts to increase participation, and arguments over who qualifies as a supporter or opponent.

Administration

The wiki's administrative situation is currently recorded as "no one i guess". This description is humorous rather than a verified statement about the actual management of the website.
