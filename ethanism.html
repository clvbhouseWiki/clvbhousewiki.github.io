Ethanism is a community-defined ideology centered on Ethan and the Clvbhouse community. The term is used within the community to describe its identity, supporters, symbols, and associated material.

Ethanism has developed its own collection of flags, emblems, propaganda-style artwork, videos, and community figures. These materials are documented in this article's symbols and gallery sections.

Editorial note: This page documents Ethanism as it is described within the Clvbhouse community. Community titles, affiliations, and historical descriptions should be understood in that context.

Overview

Ethanism is associated with Ethan and the Clvbhouse community. Its identity is expressed through community-made graphics, flags, emblems, videos, and references to people involved in the community.

The Ethanist collection includes multiple flag designs and emblem variations. Some works are presented as propaganda-style material, while others celebrate individual members or the community's identity.

Leadership

Within the community's stated organizational structure, Camemac and ARB are identified as leaders. Saar Sparky and Sancho are identified as chairmen.

Ethan is the central figure associated with Ethanism. The ideology's name, symbolism, and related media refer back to Ethan and the wider Clvbhouse community.
