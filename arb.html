ARB, also known as Assault Rifle Bear or Yassin, is a member of the Clvbhouse community associated with Ethanism.

ARB is identified as one of the leaders of Ethanism, alongside Camemac. He is Egyptian and is described as a Coptic Orthodox Christian.
