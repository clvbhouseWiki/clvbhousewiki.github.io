Camemac is a member of the Clvbhouse community and the founder of Ethanism. He is from Scotland and is associated with the ideology's development and organization.

Camemac is identified as one of the leaders of Ethanism, alongside ARB. His flag, named camemac_flag_25th_december.png, is displayed in the infobox.
