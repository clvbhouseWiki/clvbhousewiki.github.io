Meaning

The expression introduces a new point or adds information to an existing discussion. It can introduce a reminder, a correction, an observation, or a completely unrelated question.

BY THE WAY BY THE WAY BY THE WAY The words have not changed meaning. Your browser is just having a wonderful time.

Usage

The phrase is commonly placed at the beginning of a sentence and is usually followed by a comma. It can also appear near the end of a sentence.

  • By the way, the meeting starts at six.
  • I found your notebook, by the way.
  • By the way, why is this sentence vibrating?
