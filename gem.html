A gem is a term used in the Clvbhouse Wiki to describe an article, creation, image, or other piece of community content considered especially good, memorable, or valuable.

The label is a mark of appreciation. Gem-worthy content can include notable community figures, exceptional artwork, memorable edits, and articles that deserve extra attention.

The familiar blue diamond is used as a visual symbol for gems. On this wiki, a page marked as a gem receives a shiny cyan banner near the top of the article.

Etymology

The word gem comes from the idea of a precious or valuable object. In online community slang, it can be used figuratively for something considered exceptionally good, entertaining, or worthy of recognition.

On Clvbhouse Wiki, the diamond icon and cyan lettering distinguish gem-marked articles from ordinary pages.

Variants

The following names are possible gem-themed labels and categories for community use.

Gemeralds

A green-themed gem variant, inspired by emeralds.

Tanzanite

A blue-violet gem variant inspired by the mineral tanzanite.

Ruby

A red-themed gem variant inspired by rubies.

Gemuby

A playful name combining the idea of a gem with ruby.

Seetherald

A community-style gem name inspired by emerald-themed wordplay.

Topaz

A golden or yellow gem variant inspired by topaz.

Peak gems

Peak gems are pieces of content considered especially deserving of recognition. A gem badge may be used on articles that stand out within the community.
