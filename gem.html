GEM is a term used within the CLVBHOUSE Wiki's gemstone collection. Its name is represented by a bright cyan appearance, with a glossy surface and a luminous glow. This article documents GEM and several related gemstone names.

Overview

The gemstone names below have separate colors and visual identities. Their animated highlights are decorative effects applied to the text; the rest of the article retains a conventional encyclopedia layout.

GEM

GEM is represented in bright cyan and ice-blue tones. A moving highlight travels across the letters, creating the appearance of a polished, reflective crystal.

The cyan color distinguishes GEM from the red appearance of GEMUBY and RUBY, as well as the green appearance of GEMRALD.

GEMUBY

GEMUBY uses a ruby-red color scheme. Its glossy red surface, pale reflective highlight, and red glow give it a distinct appearance from the cyan GEM lettering.

RUBY

RUBY is presented in vivid crimson and scarlet tones. Its text effect uses a moving highlight and a soft red glow to suggest a polished red gemstone.

GEMRALD

GEMRALD is represented by emerald-green lettering. Its highlights use lighter green and near-white tones, while the surrounding glow remains green.
